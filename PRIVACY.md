# Privacy Policy

Copy as Markdown by Temi converts the page you are on to Markdown and writes it to
your clipboard when you click its toolbar icon or press its shortcut.

The extension reads the content of the current tab only when you trigger it. The
conversion happens in your browser. The extension does not collect, store,
transmit, or sell any data, and it has no accounts, analytics, or telemetry.

What you do with the copied text, such as pasting it into an LLM, is between you
and that service.

Questions: open an issue at
https://github.com/usetemi/copy-page-as-markdown/issues.
